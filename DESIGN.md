## Brand & Style

The design system embodies the tension between traditional literary reflection and precise, modern strategic consultancy. Tailored for Mehjabin Badhon (alias Drohi — Rebel), the visual language bridges thoughtful essayist depth with high-impact corporate advising. 

The aesthetic is grounded in **Editorial Tactility & Refined Minimalism**:
- **Tone & Emotion:** Unflinching, intellectual, evocative, grounded, and authoritative. It evokes the feeling of deliberate ink pressed into heavy deckle-edged paper, married with modern structural precision.
- **Visual Tension:** Classical editorial layouts with ample margins and traditional typographic hierarchy are contrasted with razor-sharp data containers, disciplined rule lines, and purposeful whitespace.
- **Cultural Duality:** English analytical clarity is mirrored seamlessly with authentic, dignified Bengali literary typesetting.

## Colors

The palette operates on organic, paper-and-pigment foundations rather than cold digital gamuts:

- **Primary (`#1A1612` - Deep Carbon Ink):** Applied to primary text, primary button fills, sharp line accents, and high-impact typographic headers.
- **Secondary (`#C45A3C` - Burnt Terracotta / Rebel Rust):** Used for focal highlights, links, active navigational states, and editorial pull-quote accents.
- **Tertiary (`#6B635A` - Muted Umber Gray):** Used for captions, secondary subheadings, metadata, and non-critical supportive copy.
- **Neutral Canvas (`#F8F5F0` - Aged Parchment):** The primary backdrop color across all viewports, providing a warm, non-glare, tactile surface.
- **Surface Elevation (`#FFFFFF` - Crisp Card White):** Reserved for elevated cards, editorial reading panes, and interactive input backdrops.
- **Soft Accent Wash (`#F0DDD5`):** Subtle backdrops for badges, quotes, callouts, and hover states.
- **Borders & Dividers (`#E5DFD8`):** Deliberate, low-contrast structural rules that mimic fine editorial book layout guides.

## Typography

The typographic hierarchy pairs high-contrast transitional serifs with a clean, structural grotesque:

- **Headings & Pull-quotes:** Rendered in **Playfair Display** (English) and **Noto Serif** (Bengali). Titles preserve deliberate literary poise, utilizing negative letter spacing on larger sizes to maintain tension.
- **Body & Editorial Copy:** English long-form content is set in **Inter** for optimal digital legibility and consulting clarity. Bengali text leverages **Noto Serif** (with CSS fallback to `Noto Serif Bengali`), demanding an expanded line-height of 1.75 to 1.9 to prevent matra and kar clashes.
- **Labels & Meta:** Micro-copy, publication dates, read times, and tags are set in crisp uppercase Inter with wide tracking (`0.06em` to `0.08em`) to echo academic catalog indexing.

## Layout & Spacing

The layout is structured around an asymmetrical editorial grid that privileges generous margins and intentional reading measures:

- **Grid Architecture:** 12-column responsive fluid grid on desktop (max width: `1280px`), condensing to 8 columns on tablet and 4 columns on mobile.
- **Reading Measure:** Long-form thought leadership and essays are locked to a strict maximum content width of `680px` (or 7 columns centered) to preserve optimal typographic cadence.
- **Section Breaks:** Spaced using wide vertical pauses (`space-xl` multiplied for large hero transitions), punctuated by hairline dividing borders (`#E5DFD8`) rather than aggressive solid backgrounds.
- **Consulting Blocks:** Strategic case studies and advisory frameworks utilize a structured 2-to-3 column modular breakdown with strict column gutters (`1.5rem`).

## Elevation & Depth

Visual hierarchy rejects artificial drop shadows in favor of **Tonal Paper Layers and Architectural Inset Lines**:

- **Flat & Grounded:** All components rest flush against the parchment (`#F8F5F0`) canvas or on raised card surfaces (`#FFFFFF`).
- **Low-Contrast Hairlines:** Depths and bounds are communicated strictly through 1px border lines using `#E5DFD8`.
- **Delicate Ambient Drift:** For floating menus or elevated modal dialogues, avoid dark black drops. Use a warm, highly diffused ambient shadow: `0px 12px 32px -4px rgba(26, 22, 18, 0.06)`.
- **Tactile State Shifts:** Interactive cards react without heavy elevation jumps; instead, cards respond on hover with a crisp border shift from `#E5DFD8` to `#C45A3C` and an underlying micro-tint of `#F0DDD5`.

## Shapes

The design system utilizes **Soft (`0.25rem` / `4px`)** edge styling. 

- This slight, intentional bevel softens the starkness of classic brutalism while preserving the crisp, bookish discipline of high-end print editorial.
- Chips, metadata badges, and form elements follow the same conservative `0.25rem` corner radius. Completely circular or pill shapes are avoided to keep the visual tone serious, architectural, and literary.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Primary:** Background `#1A1612`, text `#F8F5F0`, corner radius `4px`, padding `0.75rem 1.5rem`. Typography is `label-md` (uppercase, tracked). On hover: shifts background to `#C45A3C`.
- **Secondary / Outline:** Background transparent, 1px solid border `#1A1612`, text `#1A1612`. On hover: subtle fill of `#F0DDD5` with border shifting to `#C45A3C`.
- **Text Action:** Inline terracotta text (`#C45A3C`) paired with a persistent 1px underline offset by `4px`, transitioning opacity on hover.

### Chips & Badges
- Structural container with a `4px` radius, background `#F0DDD5`, text `#C45A3C`, uppercase `label-sm` tracking. Used for consulting domains, advisory topics, and publication categories.

### Editorial & Consulting Cards
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px perimeter border `#E5DFD8`, radius `4px`, padding `2rem`.
- Header section separates the category chip and read-time via a subtle top row, followed by a `headline-md` title and `body-md` excerpt.

### Form Inputs
- Background `#FFFFFF`, 1px border `#E5DFD8`, text `#1A1612`, placeholder `#6B635A`.
- Active focus state: 1px border `#C45A3C` with zero shadow ring. Label sits above input in `label-sm` uppercase.

### Checkboxes & Radios
- Square / circular controls with a 1px border of `#6B635A`. Checked state fills with `#1A1612` housing a crisp white icon indicator.

### Pull Quotes & Rebel Accents
- Left-bordered with a deliberate 3px vertical rule in `#C45A3C`, indented by `1.5rem`.
- Set in italicized Playfair Display or Noto Serif Bengali with a deep ink finish.
